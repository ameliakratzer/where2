# Where2

Where2 is a social travel-planning app that brings recommendations from friends and family into one place.

Right now, travel inspiration is scattered across TikTok, group chats, and saved Google Maps, making it difficult to actually organize a trip. Where2 lets users discover places through people they trust, find experiences that match their interests, and turn saved places into a day-by-day itinerary.
